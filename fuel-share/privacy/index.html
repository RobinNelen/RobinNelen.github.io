Fuel Share — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · App: Fuel Share (iOS) · Bundle ID: com.robin.FuelShare · Apple ID: 1161886929 · Publisher: Robin Nelen / Nelen Consult

Fuel Share is a local fuel-cost calculator. You enter trip numbers (distance, consumption, fuel price, tip, toll, and number of people) and the app calculates fuel volume, total cost, and cost per person. Units can be Eur (km, l/100km, €/l) or US (miles, mpg, $/gallon).

Data collection

Fuel Share does not collect personal data. This matches the App Store privacy nutrition label declaration Data Not Collected and the app’s PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy file, which declares no tracking, no collected data types, and no accessed APIs that require privacy reasons.

  • No account, sign-in, or profile
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No network requests by the app for telemetry or content
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or Health data access
  • Numbers you type stay on your device for the calculator session; the app does not upload them

Children

The app is a utility calculator and does not target children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties because it does not collect data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this same URL. Material changes that affect data practices would also be reflected in the App Store privacy nutrition label.
